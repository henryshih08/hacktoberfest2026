SOURCES & METHOD

Evidence comes first.

Two NASA Earth Observatory images acquired by Landsat 5 on October 22, 1984 and January 12, 2009. Images by Jesse Allen and Robert Simmon using USGS data.

The three regions were visually reviewed for this demo. Their outlines indicate areas of interest, not measured change boundaries. No automated detection, area estimates, or ground validation are included.

The images have matching extents and use the same display geometry. October and January conditions differ: color changes alone do not establish vegetation loss. At roughly 30 meters per sensor pixel, individual buildings cannot be reliably classified.
